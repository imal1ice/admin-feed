DeFi control surface, at a glance

admin-feed · a neutral chain-read reference by ownerwatch

A neutral, chain-read reference: for each protocol, what on-chain state says about how its contracts can be changed — whether changes pass through a timelock, whether the contract is upgradeable, what kind of address controls it, and when its admin surface last changed. Generated with ownerwatch.

Chain view, not a verdict. This shows what contract state says, not whether a setup is safe or risky. An address is not how its key is held (an externally-owned account may be a hardware or MPC wallet; a multisig's signers may be one organisation). A blank cell means this read did not resolve that fact from this entry point — never that the fact is absent. This is a neutral public-good reference, not a ranking or a judgment of any team.

Method

Each core-contract row is ownerwatch probe <governance-entry> --rpc <rpc> --json, walking owners, proxy admins and roles to the controller that can change the contract, and reading any timelock delay on the way. The governance delays are read the same way from the registry's timelock contracts; the last-change column is the latest event on those contracts in the live feed. The generator is transparency_gen.py. Nothing is sent on-chain.
