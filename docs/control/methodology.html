Admin events: methodology

How the per-protocol counts at admin-feed/control (JSON) are produced. imal1ice · October 2026

What is counted

For each protocol, the governance contracts that decide over it on Ethereum mainnet: timelocks, governors, voting apps, executors and the Safes named in the protocol's own address registry. The list, with each address's source and the day watching started, is control/protocols.json. It follows the governance systems mapped in defi-dna's governance metric, and every address was re-read on-chain before it was added.

The headline reports two counts over the last 90 days, recomputed every hour:

  • Control changes: events that change who controls a contract or how. Ownership transfers, role grants and revocations, proxy upgrades and admin changes, timelock delay changes, governor parameter and timelock changes, Aragon permission and app changes, Safe signer, threshold, module and guard changes, the admin setters of Aave and Morpho, Lido's Dual Governance and timelock configuration, Rocket Pool contract upgrades, CoW solver allow-list changes, Gearbox ACL admins and 0x Settler deployments. Each event counts once.
  • Governance executions: a governance decision taking effect. Executed timelock calls, executed proposals (including Lido's timelock and Rocket Pool's DAOs), executed Aragon votes, executed Aave payloads and Sky spells executed through the pause. Counted once per transaction, because one proposal usually emits several of these events.

Not counted: proposals that were created, queued or scheduled but not executed, cancellations, pauses, and routine Safe transactions.

Where a protocol shows fewer numbers, or none

  • Governed through Safes. When a protocol is governed by Safes that act directly, a Safe transaction can be a payment or an admin action, and ownerwatch does not classify it. Those protocols show control changes only.
  • Decoded since ownerwatch 0.8.0. On 2026-10-28 every governance contract's logs over the window were compared with ownerwatch's event catalog. Six systems emitted governance events ownerwatch could not decode (Sky's pause and chief, Lido's Dual Governance and Emergency Protected Timelock, Rocket Pool's DAOs, CoW's solver allow-list, the 0x Settler deployer, Gearbox's ACL), so they showed a note and no number. ownerwatch 0.8.0 decodes them, and the same comparison now accounts for every log those contracts emitted, apart from routine ones: votes, objections and vote delegation; tokens locked, freed and voted in Sky's chief; Lido Agent deposits and transfers; the per-call traces of an executed Aragon script (LogScriptCall, ScriptResult); Rocket Pool node withdrawal addresses; and the 0x deployer's ERC-721 transfers, which move a feature's token to its newest deployment alongside the Deployed event that is counted. Rocket Pool's proposal and upgrade contracts were added to the watch list through RocketStorage's on-chain registry.
  • Lido. An Aragon vote now submits a proposal to Dual Governance; the decision takes effect when the Emergency Protected Timelock executes it. Only that execution is counted, so one decision is not counted twice.
  • Sky. Executions are spells executed through the pause. Electing a new hat in the chief (lift) is a step towards an execution, like a queued proposal, and is not counted.

Dates and sources

Events come from eth_getLogs on the listed addresses, filtered to ownerwatch's event catalog (ownerwatch events prints it). The 90-day history before watching started was filled with ownerwatch backfill over the same addresses. Every date is the block's own timestamp. Every counted event links to its transaction, so any number can be checked on Etherscan.

How to read it

These are counts of things that happened on-chain. They are not a rating, and the feed attaches no reading to them: a high number can mean active, careful governance, and a zero can mean a system that rarely needs changing. The live stream of the same events is the admin-feed.
