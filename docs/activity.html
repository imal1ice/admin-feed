Three months of DeFi admin activity, read from the chain

imal1ice · November 2026 · admin-feed

The admin-feed watches the control contracts of major DeFi protocols — their timelocks, governors, treasuries and owner multisigs — and records every admin-surface event the block it happens. Between 2026-07-30 and 2026-11-02 it logged 664 such events across Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism and Base. This is what moved. Every figure here is a count of on-chain events, each linked to its transaction in the feed; it is a transparency view, not a judgement.

Who changed their keys

The sharpest control event is a change to who holds a multisig's keys. In this window 14 of them were recorded, across 6 protocols whose Safes guard real money:

  • the Balancer treasury Safe swapped a signer (10 Sep);
  • the Morpho owner Safe added a signer and raised its threshold to 6 (22 Sep);
  • the Yearn “Brain” multisig removed two signers (15 Oct);
  • the Aave Risk Council Safe swapped a signer (22 Oct);
  • the Curve Emergency DAO Safe reshuffled two seats in one day (29 Oct);
  • the Lido Reseal committee swapped a signer (30 Oct);

None of these is hidden — they are ordinary Safe AddedOwner, RemovedOwner and ChangedThreshold events, public the moment they land. The point of the feed is that someone is reading them.

The timelock is only as good as its readers

Most of the activity — 309 executed actions and 157 queued or proposed — ran through timelocks and governors: Compound's timelock, Aave's Payloads Controller, the ENS and Arbitrum DAO timelocks, Curve's ownership voting. A timelock exists so that a privileged call can be seen before it executes. That delay only protects anyone if the queue is actually watched; a queued call that no one reads is a delay in name only.

How this was read

Every event is decoded from public chain state with ownerwatch and recorded by the admin-feed, which runs hourly and publishes an RSS feed. The catalog of what it watches, and how each event is decoded, is in the methodology. Nothing here is private: every number is a public on-chain event you can open in a block explorer from the feed.
